[ FAQ ]

Frequently asked questions.

Need more detail? Reach out and our team will help.

Ask a question
Which countries do you work in?
Enabled Veterans is global. We started with a focus on North America and are building toward veterans and employers in any country. The barriers are similar everywhere, so the model travels.
Who can join as a veteran?
Any veteran looking for work, with a particular focus on those living with a service-connected or other disability. You do not need a formal diagnosis on file to start.
What does it cost employers?
Reach out and we will walk you through options based on your size and goals. Most workplace accommodations themselves cost little or nothing to put in place.
How are accommodations handled?
We agree accommodations before the first interview, not after an offer. We then support both sides through onboarding so nothing is left to chance.
How is this different from a general job board?
A job board lists roles. We fix the hiring process itself, match on skills rather than keywords, and stay involved through the hire so it lasts.
